description=[h1]Pick anything to build. Get the whole plan.[/h1]
description=A smelting furnace takes a trowel, 40 stone blocks, and concrete, and each of those has its own chain. Craft Planner works the whole thing out: which tools you need, what to go gather, and every craft in the order you do it. It ticks things off live as you collect them.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-craft-planner/main/docs/planner-plan.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]How to use it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Open the crafting or build window, select what you want, and hit [b]Track[/b] next to Craft / Build.
description=[*]Or press [b]K[/b] to open the planner and type what you want to make.
description=[*]Or right-click any item and choose [b]Track how to make ...[/b].
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-craft-planner/main/docs/planner-buildwindow.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Reading the plan[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Tools (kept)[/b]: saws, hammers, trowels. You need one, and none of them get used up. Don't have one? The planner picks one you can make and adds it to the steps.
description=[*][b]Gather (N missing)[/b]: raw materials and fluids as have/need. Missing items sit at the top.
description=[*][b]Steps, in order[/b]: every craft from the bottom of the chain up, ending with your goal. Each step shows as ready, after earlier steps, or blocked on gathering.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Smart about what you already have[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]It spends what's in your bag and nearby containers first, and only real shortfalls become gathering or sub-crafts.
description=[*]Quantities scale with recipe yield: 4 planks at 3 per log means 2 logs.
description=[*]Leftovers from one batch feed later steps.
description=[*]Anything marked [b]+N more, click to switch[/b] can be clicked to cycle alternatives (nails vs screws, a different saw, another recipe).
description=[*]Missing skills and recipes you haven't learned are flagged on the step that needs them.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-craft-planner/main/docs/planner-search.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Hands off[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Make the thing and it clears itself from your plans (toggle in Mod Options).
description=[*]Right-click a goal's header to stop tracking it.
description=[*]Plans are saved on your character, so they survive relogs and server restarts.
description=[*]Rebind the K key in Options > Mods > Craft Planner.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=Client-side only. It reads recipes and your own containers and changes nothing in the world. Safe on any server.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42 only.
description=[*]Picks up recipes and buildables from other mods automatically.
description=[*][b]Neat Crafting[/b]: the Track button is the clipboard icon in the top-right corner of the recipe icon. It turns green when tracked.
description=[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
description=[*]Fluids are counted in carried and nearby containers. Rain barrels and sinks aren't counted.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Pairs well with[/h2]
description=[b]Craft Whole Stack[/b]: right-click a stack and craft all of it at once. Knock out a plan's Saw Logs x12 step in one click.
